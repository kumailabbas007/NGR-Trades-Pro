Gold trading in simple words

Gold trading means taking a position on whether gold's price will rise or fall. XAU/USD shows the price of one troy ounce of gold in US dollars.

Bid, ask and spread

The bid is the price at which you can sell; the ask is the price at which you can buy. The spread is the difference between them and is a trading cost.

Lots and leverage

A lot describes trade size. Leverage lets you control a larger exposure with less margin, but it also magnifies losses. Broker contract specifications differ.

Candlesticks

Each candle shows open, high, low and close for a selected period. A candle alone does not predict the next move.
